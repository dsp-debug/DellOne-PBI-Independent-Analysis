# About

The analysis focuses on Agriculture products that are imported and exported for a ten-year period (2013 - 2022) in Zambia.



# Key Insights

1. High fertilizer imports indicate that Zambia's agricultural productivity remains heavily tied to foreign-sourced chemical inputs. 
2. Sesame seeds and cashew nuts are key export drivers, fuelled by the rapid expansion of industrial manufacturing in Asian markets.
3. . Structural imbalance where top imports are mostly refined staples, while the top 10 exports are predominantly raw, unprocessed goods.
4. During the 2020-2021 pandemic caused high freight costs and shipping delays resulting in a "value-over-volume" trend, where the total amount spent on imports rose significantly even if the physical quantity remained stable.
5. The local currency depreciated against the dollar between 2014 and 2022. This often leads to a widening Trade Deficit as the cost of importing essential agricultural inputs such as fertilizers, and processed foods becomes significantly more expensive.
