Topic: Diabetes and Obesity Prevalence in Zambia (1990 - 2022) 
Data Source: Humanitarian Data Exchange website

Key Insights: The prevalence of obesity and diabetes have a strong positive correlation. The prevalence rates of diabetes and obesity have been increasing over the years. A larger proportion of females are diabetic compared to males. A larger proportion of diabetic males are on treatment compared to diabetic females. There is a larger proportion of obese and overweight females than males.
