# About
The analysis focuses on Agriculture products that are imported and exported for a ten-year period (2013 - 2022) in Zambia.


# Key Insights
1. Exports declined durinng draught period (2014, 2017, 2022) by K715,675,494.39, K894,765,307.05, K1,218,302,507.48. 
2. Imports only declined in 2018 by K192,298,419.52
3. The top ten products within the ten-year period are Tobacco, other raw sugar cane, oil cake, maize excluding seeds, cotton, none stemmed tobacco, sweet biscuits, maize seeds, raw sugar cane, and beet sugar.
4. The top ten products imported from 2013 to 2022 are urea, chemical fertilizer, frozen jack and horse mackerel, nitrogenous fertilizer, ammonium nitrate, other fertilizers, crude palm oil, crude soya bean oil, palm olein, and ammonium dihydrogen orthophosphate.
5. The total export, and import amount are roughly K76.65 billion, and K110.04 billion respectively. This results in a K33.39 billion deficit.
