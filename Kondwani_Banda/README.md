The objective of this report is to analyze historical exchange rate trends of the Zambian Kwacha (ZMW) against major currencies such as USD, EUR, GBP, and South African Rand (ZAR), and to determine an optimal average exchange rate target that a central bank could consider for policy guidance. I will be using a single dataset from the Bank of Zambia containing daily buying and selling average exchange rates from 2007 to 2026 and inflation datasets for both Zambia and the USA from 2006 to 2025 respectively. Therefore the model walks you through how the Kwacha is structurally weak but stabilizing, how volatility remains elevated, how inflation continues to drive depreciation and how recent appreciation may be temporary.
