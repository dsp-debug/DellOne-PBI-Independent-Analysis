The topic of my analysis was: Global demographic trends (1965- 2023): Developed vs developing countries.
The dataset I analysed was from the UN website.
The link for the data source: https://population.un.org/wpp/downloads?folder=Standard%20Projections&group=Most%20used
The key variables of my analysis were population growth rates, fertility rates, mortality rates, and life expectancy. 
This analysis is relevant because it uncovers the socioeconomic implications of demographic trends such as healthcare demand, education planning, pension systems, and labour demand between developed and developing countries.

Key insights:
A clear gap exists between the two development groups:
Fertility rates are higher in developing countries.
Life expectancy is higher in developed countries.
Mortality rates are higher in developing countries,but declining.
Global demographic patterns show a transition toward lower fertility and mortality and higher life expectancy.
While developed countries face challenges related to aging populations, developing countries are experiencing rapid changes that impact economic development, healthcare demand, and labour markets.
