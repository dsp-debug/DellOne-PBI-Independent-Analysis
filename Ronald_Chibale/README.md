My topic is on the analysis of life expectancy at birth, and health expenditure (PPP and % of GDP) of countries in the world from 2000 to 2023.
The dataset is from the World Bank.
The key insight is that there is a positive correlation between life expectancy at birth and health expenditure (PPP and % of GDP) of the countries in the world from 2000 to 2023.
That is, as % of GDP increases, life expectancy at birth increases, and as health expenditure (Per Capita, PPP) increases, life expectancy at birth increases.
