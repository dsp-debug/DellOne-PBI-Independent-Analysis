My topic is on the analysis of life expectancy at birth, GDP per capita, and health expenditure of Zambia from 1990 to 2023.
The dataset is from the World Bank and UN data portals.
The key insight is that there is a positive correlation between life expectancy at birth, GDP per capita, and health expenditure of Zambia from 1990 to 2023.
That is, as GDP per capita increases, life expectancy at birth increases, and as health expenditure increases, life expectancy at birth increases.
